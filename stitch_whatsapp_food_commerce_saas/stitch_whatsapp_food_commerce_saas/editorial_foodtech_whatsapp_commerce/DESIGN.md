## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a warm, premium, and operationally rigorous visual language tailored for modern West African culinary entrepreneurs, caterers, and food brands. It bridges high-end editorial food presentation with instantaneous conversational commerce via WhatsApp.

### Emotional Response & Personality
- **Warm & Gastronomic:** Welcoming, vibrant, and appetizing, avoiding clinical SaaS tropes without sacrificing technical rigor.
- **Reliable & Direct:** Communicates trust, quick turnaround, and seamless conversational checkout flow.
- **Editorial Precision:** Balanced typography, generous breathing room, structured receipts, and magazine-quality food presentation.

### Design Movement: Modern Warm Tactile
A hybrid of contemporary editorial food tech (Deliveroo, Shopify Storefront) and tactile conversational utility. It emphasizes crisp micro-borders, organic cream canvases, velvety terracotta accents, and ticket/thermal paper metaphors for kitchen tickets, invoices, and WhatsApp order summaries.

## Colors

The palette grounds high-energy culinary vibrance in organic, sun-baked earth tones and structured corporate utility.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Accent (`#E24A2B` - Terracotta / Jollof Red):** The culinary heartbeat. Applied to primary calls-to-action, active tabs, price highlights, and celebratory badges.
- **Secondary Accent (`#1B4D3E` - Forest Muted Green):** Grounded, premium, and authoritative. Applied to operational badges, caterer verification checkmarks, enterprise navigation bars, and status pills.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#25D366` - WhatsApp Green):** Reserved exclusively for conversational triggers, direct WhatsApp ordering triggers, order-dispatch confirmations, and chat floating actions.
- **Neutral Deep (`#1A1A1A` - Charcoal Charcoal):** Primary text and key headings. High contrast without the harshness of pure `#000000`.
- **Neutral Muted (`#555555` - Smoked Slate):** Secondary text, dish descriptions, timestamps, and input placeholders.
- **Border Surface (`#EAE8E2` - Bone White Border):** Crisp micro-borders defining cards, divider lines, and ticket perimeters.
- **Surfaces:**
  - Base Background: `#FAF9F6` (Warm Alabaster / Calico Cream) provides an organic, non-glare foundation.
  - Card / Modal Surface: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) elevated for immediate contrast against the cream backdrop.
  - Subdued / Input Surface: `#F4F3EE` (Light Sesame) for search bars, quantity adjusters, and unselected chips.

## Typography

Typography strikes a fine balance between high-end editorial display and crisp operational clarity:
- **Sora (Headings & Pricing):** Provides geometric confidence, warmth, and contemporary culinary branding. Always maintain tight letter-spacing on display scales (`-0.02em`) to retain punchiness.
- **Inter (Body, UI, & Forms):** Maximum legibility for dense menu modifiers, allergen lists, order notes, and phone number inputs.
- **JetBrains Mono (Thermal Receipts & Order IDs):** Dedicated strictly to order ticket numbers (`#KBA-8921`), live kitchen slips, and price breakdowns within WhatsApp confirmation templates.

## Layout & Spacing

The system enforces strict layout topologies based on the context of the user interaction:

### Contextual Layout Topologies
1. **The Order & Checkout Tunnel (Strict Focus):**
   - Centered container constrained between `500px` and `540px`.
   - Used for menu customizers, catering event booking wizards, instant checkout, and order status trackers.
   - Eliminates peripheral distractions on desktop while scaling cleanly to 100% viewport width with `16px` padding on mobile.
2. **The Catalog & Dish Explorer:**
   - Fixed max-width grid capped at `1140px` with `20px` gutters.
   - Mobile (`<640px`): Single column or 2-column compact grid.
   - Tablet (`640px - 1024px`): 2 to 3 columns.
   - Desktop (`>1024px`): 3 to 4 regular columns.

### Spacing Discipline
- Layout spacing strictly adheres to multiples of 4px and 8px.
- Compact vertical pacing is maintained in tickets and orders to eliminate scrolling during order configuration.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical sheet-paper layering over warm calico surfaces, combining crisp borders with barely-there diffused shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** The base background `#FAF9F6`. No border, zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Menu Cards, Dish Tiles, Inputs):** `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a `1px` solid border in `#EAE8E2` and shadow `0 1px 3px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Active Modifiers, Hovered Dishes):** Elevated cards on interaction. Shadow `0 6px 16px -4px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -2px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.03)` with border `#DDD9D0`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Bottom Order Bars, WhatsApp Modals):** Persistent sheets. Shadow `0 -4px 20px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.07)` with crisp top border.
- **Thermal Ticket Skeuomorph:** White card with perforated/scalloped edges on top or bottom, bordered by `#EAE8E2` and backed by a flat, warm ambient drop (`0 8px 24px -6px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.08)`).

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, modern curves (`roundedness: 2`) that bridge the gap between architectural precision and organic hospitality.

- **Buttons & Standard Inputs:** Set to `12px` to `14px` border radius (`rounded-xl`), creating comfortable tap targets without turning into complete pills.
- **Cards & Food Item Containers:** Set to `16px` (`rounded-2xl`).
- **Pills & Status Indicators:** Set to full radius (`9999px`) for category filter chips, operational badges, and dietary labels.
- **Thermal Tickets:** Upper corners rounded at `12px`; bottom edge uses a repeating triangular zigzag or dashed CSS mask to emulate physical receipt paper.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Dimensions & Geometry:** Strict `44px` to `48px` height with `20px` horizontal padding. Rounded to `12px` - `14px`.
- **Behavior:** Never stretched edge-to-edge on desktop layouts; buttons retain intrinsic content width or max out at `320px`. The only exception is inside mobile screens and the strict `500px-540px` checkout tunnel where full-width buttons provide effortless thumb reach.
- **Variants:**
  - *Primary (Terracotta CTA):* `#E24A2B` background, `#FFFFFF` text, subtle active press translation (`translate-y-[1px]`).
  - *WhatsApp Direct Action:* `#25D366` background, `#FFFFFF` text, embedded SVG WhatsApp icon, subtle hover shift to `#1FB855`.
  - *Secondary / Utility:* Transparent surface, `1px` border in `#EAE8E2`, `#1A1A1A` text. Hover background `#F4F3EE`.

### 2. Category Chips & Tabs
- **Filter Chips:** Height `36px`, full pill radius (`9999px`), `14px` horizontal padding.
  - *Default:* `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px` border in `#EAE8E2`, `#555555` text.
  - *Active:* `#1A1A1A` background, `#FFFFFF` text, zero border.
- **Segmented Tabs:** Inset within a `#F4F3EE` container with a sliding `#FFFFFF` indicator pill that carries a soft `shadow-sm`.

### 3. Food Catalog Cards
- Background `#FFFFFF` with `1px` border `#EAE8E2`, `16px` radius.
- Aspect ratio 4:3 or 1:1 for hero food imagery with `overflow-hidden`.
- Content stack: Sora semi-bold title, two-line truncated description in `#555555`, numeric price pill in `#E24A2B`, and a rounded plus (+) quick-add button (`36px` x `36px`).

### 4. Thermal Order Ticket Component
- Dedicated representation of caterer event orders and WhatsApp invoices.
- Paper white background (`#FFFFFF`) with dashed inner dividers (`border-dashed border-[#DDD9D0]`).
- Monospaced line items (`JetBrains Mono`), bold order totals, and a distinct QR code or WhatsApp direct sync bar anchored at the bottom.

### 5. Input Fields & Form Tunnels
- Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px` solid `#EAE8E2`, `12px` radius.
- Focus state: border color jumps to `#E24A2B` with an ambient glow ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(226, 74, 43, 0.12)`).
- Specific styling for WhatsApp phone inputs with pre-fixed country dial codes (+234, +233, +225, +44, +33) integrated directly into the input shell.

### 6. Toggle Switches & Checkboxes
- **Toggle Switches:** `26px` height x `46px` width track. Inactive track `#EAE8E2`, active track `#1B4D3E`. Thumb is crisp `#FFFFFF` with `2px` inset and `shadow-sm`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `20px` square or circle. Checked state filled with `#E24A2B` featuring a white checkmark or center pip.
