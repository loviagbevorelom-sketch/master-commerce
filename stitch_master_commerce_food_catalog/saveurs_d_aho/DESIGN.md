## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the rich, vibrant heritage of authentic Beninese gastronomy within Lomé's contemporary urban pulse. It balances artisanal culinary warmth with the sleek precision of 2025 mobile commerce.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Warmth & Appetite:** Rich, spice-inspired hues and organic surface tones trigger instant craving and hospitality.
- **Modern Craft:** Fluid transitions, generous touch areas, and pillowed corners communicate effortless digital luxury without losing street-level authenticity.
- **Trust & Fluidity:** Predictable visual hierarchies, frictionless ordering flows, and soft ambient feedback instill total confidence during ordering and mobile payments.

### Design Movement
**Warm Contemporary Tactile**: A fusion of organic minimalism and soft depth. Flat surfaces are replaced with warm, creamy paper tones, floating elevated food cards, and micro-diffused ambient shadows that simulate natural West African daylight.

## Colors

The color palette captures the culinary identity of Benin: fresh culinary greens (crin-crin, gboma), fiery pepper purees (piment vert, piment rouge), and charred palm earth tones.

### Light Mode (Default)
- **Background (`canvas`):** `#f7f6f3` (Warm organic linen)
- **Surface (`card` / `modal`):** `#ffffff` (Pure crisp white)
- **Primary:** `#16a34a` (Fresh herb green)
  - Dark state / Hover / Active: `#15803d`
  - Subtle tint / Active chips: `#f0fdf4`
  - Vibrant accent: `#4ade80`
- **Secondary (Culinary Accent):** `#f97316` (Sunlit palm orange)
- **Tertiary / Promo Accent:** Gradient from `#f97316` to `#ef4444` (Fiery chili gradient for deal badges, urgent alerts, and discount ribbons)
- **Text & Structure:**
  - Primary Text: `#1c1917` (Stone 900)
  - Secondary Text: `#78716c` (Stone 500)
  - Subtle Borders: `#e7e5e4` (Stone 200)

### Dark Mode
- **Background (`canvas`):** `#12100e` (Deep charcoal ember)
- **Surface (`card` / `modal`):** `#1e1b18` (Smoked terracotta)
- **Primary:** `#22c55e` (Luminous herbal green)
  - Subtle tint / Active states: `rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.12)`
- **Secondary:** `#fb923c` (Luminous papaya orange)
- **Text & Structure:**
  - Primary Text: `#f5f5f4` (Stone 100)
  - Secondary Text: `#a8a29e` (Stone 400)
  - Subtle Borders: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`

## Typography

This design system relies entirely on **Outfit**, an approachable, geometric neo-grotesque that communicates modern technological edge while retaining open, hospitable counters and friendly curves.

- **Numerals:** Tabular figures are enforced for currency amounts (`XOF` / `FCFA`) to maintain alignment across cart calculations, delivery estimations, and receipts.
- **Display Weights:** Use `700` (Bold) sparingly for brand slogans and dish hero headings.
- **Editorial Legibility:** Descriptions for traditional dishes (e.g., Amiwo au poulet bicyclette, Tchinkounmé, Sauce Gombo) use `body-md` with relaxed vertical rhythm to maintain rapid scanning while scrolling on the move.

## Layout & Spacing

A **mobile-first fluid container** pattern anchors the application, optimized for one-thumb interaction in handheld conditions across Lomé.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column vertical stream or 2-column compact card grid. Fixed margins of `16px` (`margin-mobile`), internal gutters of `12px` (`gutter-mobile`). Bottom thumb-reach zones dictate navigation placement.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid grid, `24px` page margins, `16px` gutters. Two-column split layout with menu listings on the left and sticky order review on the right.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column fixed-max layout (`1200px` container max-width), centered on the viewport with `32px` (`margin-desktop`) padding and `24px` (`gutter-desktop`) gutters.

### Rhythm & Safe Areas
- Vertical rhythm follows an `8px` baseline grid, scaling to `4px` for tight badge alignments.
- Floating checkout bars and dynamic drawers respect hardware safe-area insets (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`) with a minimum `16px` buffer above native navigation bars.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved without heavy, rigid outlines. The design system mimics diffuse tropical light bouncing off matte stoneware.

### Shadow Tiers (Light Mode)
- **Level 0 (Flat):** Used for background and non-interactive sections.
- **Level 1 (Surface / Dish Card):** `0 8px 24px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`. Subtle, buoyant float that isolates food imagery cleanly from `#f7f6f3`.
- **Level 2 (Active Drawer / Sticky Bottom Bar):** `0 16px 32px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Cart Overview):** `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.16)`.

### Dark Mode Elevation
Shadows become invisible against dark backgrounds; elevation is instead rendered via tonal layering:
- Canvas: `#12100e`
- Resting Cards: `#1e1b18` with subtle border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`
- Modals / Bottom Sheets: `#27231f` with border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`

### Motion & Micro-interactions
- Transitions strictly employ gentle spring easing: `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` over `200ms` to `350ms`.
- Tapping a dish card induces a subtle press scale: `scale(0.98)`.

## Shapes

The core geometry is warm and rounded (`roundedness: 2`), expressing the organic profile of traditional earthenware calabashes and polished wooden mortars.

- **Dish & Section Cards:** Exactly `20px` border-radius (`rounded-[20px]`).
- **Pills & Chips:** Full circle (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) for dietary tags, category selectors, and quantitative stepper buttons.
- **Action Buttons:** `14px` border-radius for secondary controls; pill (`9999px`) for primary sticky order triggers.
- **Input Fields:** `14px` border-radius for smooth, ergonomic thumb placement.
- **Badges & Micro-tags:** `8px` roundedness to prevent visual sharpness in small clusters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Commander", "Valider le Panier"):** Full pill (`rounded-full`), height `52px`, background `#16a34a` (dark: `#22c55e`), label color `#ffffff`, font `label-lg`. Active tap scale `0.97`.
- **Secondary CTA:** Background `#f0fdf4` (dark: `rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.12)`), text color `#15803d` (dark: `#4ade80`), height `48px`, `rounded-[14px]`.
- **Cart Stepper Button (+ / -):** `36px` circular button (`rounded-full`), light gray or herb-tint surface, haptic scale on tap.

### Chips & Category Selectors
- Horizontal auto-scrolling rail.
- **Inactive:** `#ffffff` surface, text `#78716c`, border `1px solid #e7e5e4`, height `36px`, padding `0 16px`, `rounded-full`.
- **Active:** Background `#16a34a`, text `#ffffff`, shadow `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.35)`.

### Cards (Product / Dish)
- Surface `#ffffff` (dark: `#1e1b18`), border-radius `20px`, shadow `0 8px 24px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.
- **Image Treatment:** Aspect ratio 4:3 or 1:1, corner radius `16px` inset or nested top flush `20px 20px 0 0`. Image lazy-loads with a warm oatmeal placeholder shimmer.
- **Information Flow:** Dish name (`headline-sm`), brief description (`body-sm`), trailing price tag (`price-tag`), and floating quick-add circular button (`+`).

### Promo Badges
- Continuous gradient background: `linear-gradient(135deg, #f97316 0%, #ef4444 100%)`.
- Text: `#ffffff`, `label-sm`, uppercase, letter spacing `0.04em`.
- Padding: `4px 10px`, border-radius `8px`, positioned top-left over dish media.

### Form Inputs & Delivery Selectors
- Height `48px`, background `#ffffff` (dark: `#1e1b18`), border `1.5px solid #e7e5e4` (dark: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`), border-radius `14px`.
- **Focus state:** Border color `#16a34a`, glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.15)`.
- Neighborhood selector pre-populated with Lomé districts (Tokoin, Bè, Agoè, Nyékonakpoè, Aflao) via frictionless search dropdowns.

### Lists & Checkout Breakdown
- Clean dividers with `#f7f6f3` or `1px solid #e7e5e4`.
- Order items display thumbnail (`48x48px`, `rounded-lg`), item count pill, dish title, dynamic customizations (e.g., "Piment modéré, extra poisson frit"), and trailing currency sum.
